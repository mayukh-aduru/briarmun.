Join the Team

No experience, no application, no tryout. Just show up to a Wednesday meeting: here’s what to expect.

When

Alternate Wednesdays

4:30–5:30 PM in Room 202.

Who

Every grade

Freshmen through seniors, whether it’s your first club or your fourth year of MUN.

Bring

Just yourself

A notebook helps, but the only real requirement is curiosity about how the world works.

Getting Started

Three steps to your first committee

  1. Come to any meeting before dues are finalized

    Pick any date on the schedule and head to Room 202 at 4:30. No RSVP needed.

  2. Fill out the interest form

    It puts you on our email list for reminders and conference sign-ups. Jump to the form

  3. Pay club dues before October 14th

    Dues are paid through SchoolCash Online.
